# Governance

## Who holds what

A Swiss non-profit association holds the name, the domain, the repository and the signing keys. Its statutes fix three rules that no one, the founder included, can change alone: Eruko is free for every learner, shows no advertising and keeps no learner data.

## Release rights

At least three people can make a release. A person gains release rights by the judgement shown in their work on the project, on the maintainers' vote, and loses them by the rule the statutes set.

Before 1.0.0, someone other than the founder makes a release alone, to prove the project does not depend on one person.

## The founder

The founder leaves the top office on the date written in the statutes.
