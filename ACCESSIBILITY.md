# Accessibility

Eruko is built to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA, in every theme, by keyboard, with a screen reader, at 400% zoom and with reduced motion.

Every screen is checked by axe in the browser tests. That finds only part of what blocks a learner, so no unit is labelled Ready ([`STATUS.md`](STATUS.md)) until disabled learners, paid for their time, have studied it in a testing round and found no barrier left open.

Report a barrier as an issue: the page, what you used (browser, screen reader, zoom) and what stopped you.
