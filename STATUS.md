# Status

Each unit is labelled with one of three words.

- **Draft**: the course is not complete.
- **Testing**: the course is complete and in use, and has not yet passed the testing round below.
- **Ready**: disabled learners, paid for their time, studied it in a testing round and found no barrier left open.
