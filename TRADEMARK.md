# The name

Anyone may copy, change and share Eruko under its licence ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)).

A changed copy that you share with others takes another name. The name "Eruko" and its logo, the curvilinear triangle in [`src/shell/mark.svg`](src/shell/mark.svg), mark the copy its maintainers release, so a learner always knows which one they have.

Saying what your copy is built on ("based on Eruko") is always allowed.
